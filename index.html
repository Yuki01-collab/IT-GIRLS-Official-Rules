00 · INTRODUCTION

The standard
we uphold.

These rules establish the standards expected of every member of the IT-GIRLS Sorority. They exist to ensure that every member is heard, respected, treated fairly, and able to participate in a safe and supportive community.

THE RULEBOOK

Specific Rules

⌕

Every member has the right to express their opinions and concerns.

Everyone must be given an equal opportunity to participate in discussions and organizational decisions.

Members must respect differences in background, beliefs, personality, and experiences.

Bullying and discrimination are strictly prohibited.

Every member should have an equal chance to take on leadership roles, join projects, and receive opportunities.

Members are also expected to do their part and contribute to the Sorority's activities.

Members should support and look out for one another while creating a safe and comfortable community.

Hazing, violence, and intimidation are not allowed.

FINAL PROVISION

The standard
starts with us.

Every member of IT-GIRLS is expected to uphold these rules and contribute to an environment built on equality, respect, fairness, sisterhood, safety, and trust.
